Support

Need a hand with Bitty City? We're happy to help.

Common questions

How do I invite friends? Open your city, tap Add friend, and share the 6-letter code. Up to 3 friends can join a city.

Why didn't my city grow? A build only advances on days when everyone in the crew marks the goal done. Miss a day and an asteroid may strike; a full week of inactivity brings a meteor.

My streak broke — can I fix it? A recently broken streak can be repaired with one tap, and streak freezes bank automatically as you build.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. This removes your data and leaves every city (founded cities are deleted for all members).
